The Veetr kit offer, preserved

Kit orders are paused. This page keeps the former veetr.com offer available while Veetr works toward a compliant, documented product.

Kit information

You can still build Veetr from the open hardware files and standard parts today.

Veetr.com previously described a made-to-order kit and a complete do-it-yourself parts route. That storefront is being retired in favor of one project website, but its practical information remains available here.

The former complete-kit offer is not currently accepting orders. Prices, shipping estimates, and sales terms below are retained for reference and may change before finished hardware becomes available again.

For the current product plan, including the work needed before a distributable batch, see Get Your Veetr. For the supported route today, use the build guide and hardware documentation.

Orders are paused

The former offer used Stripe for payment and email confirmation for shipping details. No finished kit can currently be ordered through this site.

Former shipping policy

Worldwide shipping was included

The early-stage offer described free worldwide shipping to keep ordering simple, even when that reduced project margins.

It stated delivery could take up to 12 weeks because kits were assembled by hand without a stock reserve or mass-production capital. The early-adopter section described the same estimate as 6–12 weeks.

These estimates are preserved for reference and will need to be confirmed again before any future offer.

Why the kit existed

Freedom was the product

Professional components

The former offer described the ESP32, GPS, and sensors as professional-grade parts also used in commercial marine products.

Massive cost savings

The former offer compared DIY component costs with device-level certification costs of $20,000–50,000+ and described equivalent functionality at 10–20% of the commercial price.

True ownership

Schematics, firmware, PCB files, and enclosure designs stay open, repairable, and adaptable.

Learn and customize

Builders can understand the system, add features, integrate other equipment, and share improvements.

Early-adopter program

Sailors as partners and contributors

The former offer invited early users to help shape a young project rather than buy a finished mass-market instrument.

Shape the product

Sailors can test Veetr in real conditions and influence later iterations with honest feedback.

Special pricing

The former offer described early-adopter pricing with worldwide shipping included and no hidden fees.

Made to order

Each kit was to be sourced and assembled individually, with a stated 6–12 week lead time.

Full support

The offer included email access to the development team and the GitHub community forum.
